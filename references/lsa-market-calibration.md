# Market & Trade Calibration Reference

Use this to calibrate expectations in the diagnosis and the "honest timeline" section. All figures are directional benchmarks from agency blogs and forum testing (2024–2026), not Google-published — re-verify seasonally, and search the web for current CPLs if precision matters.

## Market-size calibration

- **Small city / single-town markets:** demand ceiling is real — zero-lead days can be demand, not rank. Play: tight core-area + first-ring towns; don't judge on daily volume, judge weekly/monthly.
- **Mid-size metro:** balanced; top-3 usually reachable in 1–2 quarters with review velocity + answer rate.
- **Saturated major metro (esp. roofing, HVAC):** top-3 hold hundreds to thousands of reviews and years of lead history. LSA is a 2+ quarter trust campaign; supplement with Search/PMax for interim volume. Anything promising top-3 in 30 days is snake oil — say so.

## Review-gap timeline heuristic

- Gap < 3x top-3 median: catchable in ~1 quarter at 4–8 reviews/month.
- Gap 3–10x: 2 quarters, sustained velocity.
- Gap 10x+: 6–12 months; set expectations in writing and run supplementary channels.

## Seasonality flags

- Roofing: storm season (spring hail, hurricane season in Gulf/FL) massively spikes demand AND competition.
- HVAC: first heat wave / first freeze.
- Garage door: mild seasonality; spring/fall bump.
- Judge account changes over 2+ week windows; never during/immediately after a demand spike.

## Ranking-factor weighting (community consensus, 2025–2026)

1. Responsiveness (answer rate ≥95%, <60s message response; 90s+ responses saw 30–50% impression-share drops in 2026 testing)
2. Review count + velocity + rating (velocity/recency ≈ as important as totals for newer accounts)
3. Proximity to searcher
4. Profile completeness + photos (CTR lever)
5. Lead history / booking outcomes (cold-start throttle for new accounts)
6. Bid/budget (least important once sufficient — permission to spend, not a ranking signal)
